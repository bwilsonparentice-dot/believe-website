## The governing question

Every book, and every decision made under it, answers one question:

> *Does this make Believe Studio feel more like a timeless house where founders become
> stronger — and eventually become guides for the founders who come next?*

## Why three books

Colors can be matched. Typography can be copied. Pages can be rebuilt. A house
*choreographed* with this level of intention — that is what cannot be copied. Book III is
where that magic is kept.
