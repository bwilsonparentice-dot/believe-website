# The Editorial Standard

*The standard for every page of Believe Studio, set by the Threshold pass. We are
no longer designing a website. We are editing an institution. The role is
Editor-in-Chief, not architect: protect what the House already is, don't invent
more. Subtract, simplify, clarify, unify — before adding anything.*

## The three tests
Every recommendation must answer yes to all three, or it isn't made:
1. Would this make the next founder's experience more meaningful?
2. Does this feel like a House quietly beginning its story — or pretending it
   already has one?
3. Does this make the House feel more like an institution and less like a business?

## The six principles
1. **The House does not explain itself.** If something needs extensive explanation,
   it isn't ready. Curiosity is stronger than explanation.
2. **The House never borrows history.** No invented testimonials, stories, archives,
   traditions, artifacts, or institutional memory. Only promises, invitations, and
   possibilities. History will be written by real founders.
3. **Every room sounds like the same House.** One voice. *WhyBelieve* is the tuning
   fork — when a line is in doubt, measure it there.
4. **Business language leaves.** Out: engagement, client, package, deliverables,
   advisory relationship, included, services, ROI. In: belonging, conversation,
   stewardship, practice, contribution, rooms, gathering.
5. **The House trusts silence.** A platform fears silence; the House trusts it.
   Reduce words, explanation, persuasion. Let the spaces breathe.
6. **Less is usually better.** Faced with repeated explanations, negations, or
   positioning, keep the strongest and delete the rest. Confidence is quieter.

## The two jobs of every page
A page has two responsibilities, and they must not compete:
1. **Recognize the founder** — the emotional work ("is this for me?"). This is where
   the beautiful writing belongs, and where it should be tightest: beautiful
   repetition is still repetition.
2. **Make belonging tangible** — the practical clarity ("how does this actually
   work?"): cadence, room size, format, belonging terms, cost, how rooms form and
   grow. This is the architecture of trust. It is not filler and is not cut for
   being unpoetic. It is consolidated, expressed elegantly, and kept.

Editorial repetition is removed. Necessary clarity is preserved. They are not the
same thing.

## The exemplar: The Threshold
The Threshold is the reference for the whole House. Its job is not to explain
Believe Studio; it is to let a founder feel they have arrived. The House is
introduced by being entered, not described. (Pass One removed the last line that
explained the business; nothing more is done to it.)

## Working method
Recommend → explain why → wait for approval → then change. One decision at a time;
we earn each edit before the next. Never rewrite from scratch; edit. The most
mature version of the House will almost always say less, not more.

## The editorial passes
1. Threshold — the purest expression of the House. *(Done; locked.)*
2. Remove borrowed history site-wide (the "first page is blank" principle).
3. Global tense pass: claimed history → promised future → honest invitation.
4. Remove business vocabulary.
5. Remove The Stage.
6. Final coherence pass — every page belongs to the same institution.

*The goal is a House founders remember twenty years from now — not for what it
claimed to be, but for what it quietly became.*
