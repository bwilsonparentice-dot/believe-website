# Evolution Roadmap

*How the current house measures against the Constitution — and the ordered moves that
would deepen it. Evolution, not redesign. Nothing here is built yet; this is for
review.*

---

## What the house already gets right

The built World Map already embodies much of the Constitution and should be preserved:

- The **phased arrival ritual** (overture → drawn door → building) as a felt promenade.
- The **house-as-architecture** model — rooms, hallways, doorways; experience before
  explanation.
- **Distinct room content, artifacts, and the "Language of Believe Studio"** (one
  principle, one artifact, one verb per room).
- The **absent-founder art direction** — objects and light, no stock, no teams.
- **Synthesized ambience** (Tuscan dawn + door passage), living breeze, distant birds,
  dust, slow light — all gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **ImageSlot placeholders** that carry their art-direction notes — furnished rooms
  awaiting photography.
- **Structured content in `src/data.ts`** (CMS-ready), standard **React + Vite + TS**
  (no runtime lock-in), a clean production build, Vercel-deployable.
- The **brass directory footer** rebuilt with real, crisp engraved text.

## The moves, in priority order

Each move cites the Constitution section and the panel voice(s) behind it.
**[F]** marks a move that needs a founder decision before building.

### P1 — The threshold & the surfaces (the first, deepest impression)

1. **Rebuild the arrival as a real crossing** *(§13; Architect, Hospitality, Kinfolk).*
   Oak door set into a foreground limestone wall with a visible jamb and floor plane;
   the brass handle catches light and turns; warm light spills across the threshold as
   the door opens and the view moves forward. Today's dark arch reads as a silhouette
   on a field.
2. **Move "Come in." into the architecture** *(§7, §13; Kinfolk).* Rest the words on
   the light-spill at the base of the door, sized to belong to the image — not laid
   over it like signage.
3. **Retire the "SKIP" label** *(§13; Hospitality).* Auto-open after a held beat; keep
   the ritual keyboard-advanceable and reduced-motion-safe, but never frame it as an
   exit sign.
4. **Retire the floating card; differentiate the rooms** *(§4, §5; Architect, Kinfolk).*
   Move each room from a rounded, drop-shadowed panel toward flatter, fuller material
   framing with ~⅔ negative space, and give each room its own proportion and fall of
   light so procession is *felt as changing volume.*
5. **Typographic hygiene pass** *(§7; Kinfolk).* Typographic (curly) quotes and
   apostrophes everywhere; strengthen low-contrast italic set over imagery until it
   reads with ease. Low effort, high polish.
6. **The living arrival — the olive tree and the perch** *(§9a — Living World
   Principle).* Give the arrival its olive tree, and let a bird occasionally cross and
   briefly *perch* there before flying on — rare, unscripted, never on a learnable
   timer, never a distraction, fully gated by reduced-motion. The build already flies
   distant birds over the *building*; this brings a single, alive gesture to the
   *door.*

### P2 — Orientation & voice (so no one feels lost or sold to)

7. **One greeting per room** *(§5; Hospitality, Kinfolk).* Reduce stacked
   kicker + title + subtitle to a single greeting; strip tracked-caps kickers that
   caption the obvious (start with The Table).
8. **"New here? Start here."** *(§12; First-timer).* **[F]** A persistent, quiet thread
   for the overwhelmed, leading to *one* low-stakes first step — *leave a question,
   receive one thing back* — not a "book a call" cliff. *Decision needed: what is the
   one first step, and what comes back?*
9. **A felt "you are here."** *(§12; Hospitality, First-timer).* Light sense of place
   and of how far the wander goes — whispered, not chrome.
10. **Voice — resolved by the founder** *(§11).* ✓ The manifesto stands **whole (six
   lines)** as an editorial centerpiece on *The House*; *ecosystem*, *catalyst*, and
   *transformation* are **kept** as the house's chosen words; **`Founders Leading
   Founders™` keeps its ™**. Remaining voice work is only: avoid *hollow* jargon and
   don't narrate a visitor's ending for them. *(Built: The House chapter now carries
   the full narrative.)*
11. **Bring a founder's voice forward** *(§3, §4; First-timer, Exited).* Surface one
    real founder who "arrived exactly this lost" early in the wander, not only in the
    footer-linked Stories.

### P3 — The soul & the send-off (the longer work)

12. **Enact the guide arc** *(§14; Exited founder).* **[F]** Design one concrete act of
    giving-back — a founder leaves a note/object/hour that a later founder receives —
    as the payoff of The Stage, traced in the Library and Field Notes. Optionally, a
    small honest "scars" moment. *This is the biggest strategic piece and needs the
    founder's real mechanic: what is left, how, and who receives it?*
13. **A departure ritual** *(§3; Hospitality).* A closing beat that quiets the light and
    leaves *"The door stays open."* — so guests leave held, not dropped.
14. **Directional light that tracks time** *(§9; Architect).* One advancing sun — dawn
    at the door toward dusk at Private Advisory — replacing static per-room gradients.
15. **A recurring sound motif** *(§10; Hospitality).* One quiet, returning sound so the
    house feels continuous.
16. **Accessibility hardening** *(§15).* On-brand visible focus states, dialog
    semantics + focus-trap for overlays, AA contrast throughout, `alt` from the
    art-direction notes.
