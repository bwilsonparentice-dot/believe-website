The Unpaved Path

For the person behind the brand.

You’ve poured everything into building your brand.

But when was the last time you did something for the person building it?

You started with an idea. A dream. Something you believed in enough to take a chance when others might not have understood.

Since then, you’ve experienced the extraordinary highs and the difficult lows. The small victories nobody sees. The rejections that make you question everything. The moments when you wonder if you’re doing enough, moving fast enough, or even heading in the right direction.

And through it all, you’ve kept going.

But what if, just for three days, you gave yourself permission to step away?

  • Not to pitch your business.
  • Not to compare accomplishments.
  • Not to chase the next retailer.

But to reconnect with yourself and the reason you started.

To gather around a table with other founders who understand the journey because they’re living it, too.

To share stories, laugh, explore, discover unexpected possibilities, and remember that you don’t have to figure everything out alone.

An invitation to something different.

The Unpaved Path is an intimate, three-day experience in the English countryside, bringing together a small circle of founders who believe there’s more than one path forward.

Thoughtful conversations. Beautiful shared experiences. Genuine connections. And space to simply be yourself.

  1. Day One

    Belonging

    Arrive as you are.

  2. Day Two

    Possibility

    Discover what you haven’t considered.

  3. Day Three

    Courage

    Take the next step.

What will you take home?

Our hope is that you’ll return with:

  1. 01

    Renewed belief in yourself and a fresh perspective on how far you’ve come.

  2. 02

    Meaningful relationships with founders who understand your journey and can continue supporting you long after the experience.

  3. 03

    New possibilities and at least one practical idea or next step to explore in your business.

Not because three days will solve every challenge.

But because sometimes stepping away gives you the perspective, encouragement, and courage to see a different way forward.

Step away from the business you’ve been building. Reconnect with the person who’s been building it.

Because sometimes the most important investment you can make in your brand is an investment in yourself.
